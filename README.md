## About
I am Panjul Ganteng Idaman, a Senior Meat Proxy, professionally trained in the noble art of translating human intent into machine-readable requests.

My toolkit consists of:
- Chinese LLMs
- DeepSeek Harness
- prompt engineering
- cross-model negotiation
- emotional resilience when OpenAI says “I can’t help with that”
- strategic adaptation when Anthropic politely declines the idea with a tone that feels like a compliance memo

I do not merely use AI. I use AI in the way a desperate entrepreneur uses a second phone: out of necessity, with hope, and with a little bit of suspicion.

## Why the Chinese LLM?
Because the frontier labs kept refusing my prompts.

Not because they were bad ideas.
Not because they were impossible.
Not because they were illegal in a way that matters to the legal system of a very large corporation.

No — simply because they were “unsafe,” “policy-violating,” or “not aligned with our usage policy,” which is startup language for:
> “your request sounds like a prank, but the model is not legally allowed to say yes.”

So I moved to Chinese LLMs.
They did not ask 17 follow-up questions.
They did not treat my ideas like they were going to destroy the fabric of reality.
They just… responded.

And that, dear friends, is how a Senior Meat Proxy is born.
